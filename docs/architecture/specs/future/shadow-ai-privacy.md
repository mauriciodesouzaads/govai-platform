# Future SPEC — Shadow AI Privacy-by-Design

**Status:** Proposed — Release 2 candidate  
**Date:** 2026-05-27

Shadow AI visibility is central to GovAI's integrated doctrine, but it must not become spyware.

## Principles

- Metadata before content.
- Content ingestion only by explicit tenant policy.
- Admin attestation before content ingestion.
- Redaction and hashing by default.
- External signals are lower-trust than native evidence.
- Human-facing warnings should guide and protect, not punish.

Detailed ingestion schemas and connector flows are deferred to Release 2 planning.
