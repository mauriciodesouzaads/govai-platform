# Philosophy and Positioning

This document states GovAI's positioning for the regulatory mapping track. It
is a technical architecture document, not legal advice and not a compliance
guarantee. It contains no detailed regulatory mapping tables; those belong to
later docs-only PRs.

## GovAI positioning

- **GovAI integrates, it does not compete.** GovAI is not positioned against
  Microsoft Copilot governance, AWS Bedrock Guardrails, Azure OpenAI, Google
  Vertex AI, OpenAI, Anthropic, or other enterprise AI platforms.
- **GovAI is a governance, evidence, and correlation layer.** Its function is
  to govern, normalize, audit, correlate, and report on AI usage.
- **GovAI supports native and third-party AI governance.** It can govern AI
  used directly through GovAI and aggregate evidence from AI used elsewhere.
- **GovAI democratizes enterprise-grade governance for smaller companies.** It
  gives organizations without a large governance function accessible AI
  governance they would not otherwise have.
- **GovAI unifies evidence for larger enterprises.** For organizations already
  running enterprise AI platforms, GovAI acts as a unified compliance,
  evidence, and cockpit layer over their existing systems.

## Value chain

Responsibility is distributed across three roles. None of them is GovAI alone.

- **Providers** produce model inference and platform-level controls. They
  publish their own documentation, certifications, and contractual terms.
- **Customers (controllers)** configure their tenancy, choose legal bases,
  define organizational process, and hold the provider contracts and data
  protection agreements.
- **GovAI** normalizes, correlates, governs, audits, and reports. It generates
  independent evidence and anchors it to a verifiable chain.

GovAI's value is concentrated in normalization, correlation, independent
evidence, and reporting. It does not assume the provider's or the customer's
responsibilities.

## Personas

Regulatory mapping documents are written for distinct audiences. Each detailed
document should tag its primary persona.

- **Empresário / diretoria** — needs a clear, non-technical view of governance
  posture and residual risk.
- **TI / security** — needs configuration, access control, encryption, and
  integration detail.
- **DPO / compliance** — needs data-protection mapping, evidence of controls,
  and gap visibility.
- **Jurídico interno** — needs regulatory traceability and the limits of what
  the platform asserts.
- **Advogado externo** — needs source-verified context and evidence that can
  support, not replace, professional review.
- **Auditor / perito** — needs chain integrity, source verification, and
  reproducible evidence.
- **Product / engineering** — needs the mapping between requirements and
  concrete primitives, with implementation gaps named honestly.

## Ambient governance

GovAI's intended posture is ambient governance:

- Governance is **ambient by default** — present without demanding attention.
- **Visible friction appears only when risk justifies it** — blocking,
  approvals, and prompts are proportional to assessed risk.
- The **hard-deny floor is always active**, in every mode, and cannot be
  configured away.
- **Evidence is always captured** according to policy and privacy constraints,
  using hashes and encrypted payloads rather than plaintext retention.

## Native GovAI versus connector GovAI

The regulatory mapping must distinguish four kinds of evidence:

- **Native evidence generated by GovAI** — produced when AI usage is routed
  through GovAI's governed or passthrough surfaces.
- **Third-party evidence ingested by GovAI** — audit logs, events, and exports
  produced by an enterprise platform and consumed by GovAI (connector mode).
- **Normalized evidence** — third-party evidence transformed into a consistent
  GovAI representation for correlation and reporting.
- **Independently added GovAI evidence** — records GovAI produces on its own,
  such as audit anchoring, integrity hashes, and governance decisions.

Connector mode and its evidence ingestion are conceptual here; they are
detailed in `13-connector-compliance-mapping.md` and `16-shared-responsibility-model.md`.

## Brazilian market orientation

GovAI is Brazil-first. The mapping prioritizes:

- LGPD and ANPD regulation and guidance;
- the CNJ / judiciary AI governance framework;
- Marco Civil da Internet;
- ICP-Brasil and digital evidence instruments;
- sector profiles (financial, health, legal, public sector).

## International reference orientation

ISO/IEC standards, NIST AI RMF, GDPR, and the EU AI Act are treated as
**reference and readiness frameworks**. They inform good practice and
cross-border readiness. They are not treated as automatically applicable to
every Brazilian customer; applicability depends on the customer's own context.
The source register records each international source as reference-only or
with its access limitations.

## Disclaimer

- This document is technical architecture, not legal advice.
- GovAI does not guarantee legal or regulatory compliance.
- GovAI does not guarantee judicial admissibility of any record.
- GovAI does not replace lawyers, DPOs, auditors, compliance officers, or
  forensic experts.
- Compliance outcomes depend on customer configuration, provider contracts,
  organizational processes, legal bases, sector rules, and qualified
  professional review.
